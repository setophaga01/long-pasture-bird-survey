Long Pasture

Eight fixed stations, counted weekly through fall migration. Cummaquid, Barnstable, Massachusetts.

What the circles measure

Only birds inside the 100 m survey radius. This is the number that compares fairly across all eight stations.

Stations

    Reading the map

    • Radius station: everything within 100 m of where I stood. The dashed ring is that radius, drawn to scale.
    • View station: a scan out over the harbor, where most birds are far past 100 m. No fixed radius.
    • Circle area is proportional to the number of birds counted.

    Counts by a single observer, 10 minutes per station. Distances estimated by eye and calibrated against orthoimagery. Data and code: GitHub.
